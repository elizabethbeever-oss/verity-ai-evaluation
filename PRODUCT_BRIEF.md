# Verity - Product brief

**Audience:** AI product teams, evaluators and learners.

**Problem:** Fluent AI-generated responses can be inaccurate or unsafe. Reviewers need a consistent way to compare quality and record their judgement.

**MVP:** Human-assigned scores on five criteria, standard/custom weights, critical-failure overrides, side-by-side comparison, synthetic examples, rationale fields and JSON export.

**Success:** A first-time reviewer can compare two answers, explain a blocked decision and export an evidence record. This is a hypothesis, not a validated outcome.

**Non-goals:** Automatic fact-checking, live model evaluation, patient data processing, clinical decision support, user accounts and database storage.

**Risks:** Reviewer bias, ambiguous criteria, unverified reference material, misleading interpretation of threshold decisions. Mitigations include criterion guidance, explicit disclaimers, evidence fields and hard safety/accuracy overrides.

**Future research:** Independent first-time user testing, inter-rater agreement, accessibility review and evaluation of alternative thresholds.
